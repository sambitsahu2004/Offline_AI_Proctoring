# Academic Integrity & Exam Conduct Policy

This policy document governs candidate expectations and integrity standards for remote and offline supervised examinations.

---

## 1. Authorized Examination Workspace
- **Environment**: Candidates must take the exam in a quiet, well-lit room, free from other people and noise distractions.
- **Surface**: The desk surface must be clean and contain only approved materials (e.g., blank scratch paper if explicitly allowed by the instructor).
- **Secondary Screens**: No additional computer screens, tablets, television screens, or smart displays are allowed to be turned on or visible in the workspace.

---

## 2. Prohibited Conduct
- **Leaving the Workspace**: Candidates are not permitted to leave their seat or step away from the camera range during the examination without explicit scheduled breaks.
- **Communication**: No talking, reading questions aloud, whisperings, or communicating with anyone via any medium is permitted.
- **Prohibited Devices**: Use of unauthorized electronics is strictly forbidden, including but not limited to:
  - Mobile/smartphones
  - Smartwatches
  - Tablets
  - Unauthorized headsets/headphones (wired or wireless)
  
---

## 3. Policy Escalation & Review Workflow
- **No Automated Failure**: Under no circumstances does the automated AI system fail a candidate or declare an absolute "cheating" verdict.
- **Review Requirement**: Any flagged deviations (Minor, Medium, High, Critical) are recorded in the event log and compiled into an integrity report.
- **Human Investigator**: A human proctor or course administrator must review the logs, the generated report, and the video segments (if flagged) to make a final qualitative decision.
- **Sanctions**: Confirmed academic dishonesty events are referred to the Academic Integrity Board, which determines disciplinary action up to course failure or suspension.
